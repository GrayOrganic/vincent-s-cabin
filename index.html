About Me

I combine mathematical reasoning and system architecture with empathetic human-centered design. My focus lies in utilizing technical mechanics to evoke specific emotional and cognitive responses, viewing interactive software as a powerful analytical medium for accessibility, education, and cultural expression.

Selected Projects

  • Voice Trail: Audio-guided 3D spatial sound delivery simulation game designed for visually impaired children.
  • Fireside Nightcast: Bio-feedback public speaking training system integrating an ADXL355 accelerometer.
  • ShareGAN Aβ-PET: AI research using ShareGAN to synthesize 3D images for Alzheimer's diagnosis.
  • Gallery of the Void: Educational action game utilizing custom shaders to teach color theory.
